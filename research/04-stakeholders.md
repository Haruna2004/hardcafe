## Key takeaways

1. **Terra's "$52M" is correct, but it is a seed total raised in three 2026 tranches, by an Abuja/Accra defence-tech company.** The tranches were $11.75M (12 Jan 2026, led by 8VC), $22M (16 Feb 2026, led by Lux Capital) and $18M (17 Aug 2026, lead not disclosed), which sums to $51.75M and is reported as $52M. There was also an earlier ~$800k pre-seed ([TechCrunch, 12 Jan 2026](https://techcrunch.com/?p=3081535); [TechCrunch, 16 Feb 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/02/16/terra-industries-raises-22-million/); [Techeconomy, 17 Aug 2026](https://techeconomy.ng/terra-industries-18m-africa-drone-factory)). Drones are one product line among towers, ground vehicles and software. Manufacturing is in Abuja (Pax-1) and Accra (Pax-2), and I found no Lagos presence.
2. **The Sterling–Miva–Café One–Terra web already exists, and the lab can be its missing Lagos "practical layer".**
   - Sterling funds up to 600 full-tuition Miva scholarships (₦2bn over 36 months) ([Sterling T&Cs, eff. 12 Jun 2025](https://sterling.ng/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/BEYOND-EDUCATION-SCHOLARSHIP-–-TERMS-CONDITIONS.pdf)).
   - Miva's student "Community Centres" in Lagos are all Café One outlets ([Miva, accessed 17 Nov 2026](https://miva.edu.ng/community-center/)).
   - In Aug 2026, Miva and Terra announced robotics, drone and VR labs across Miva study centres, starting with an Abuja pilot ([ITWeb, 3 Aug 2026](https://itweb.africa/article/nigeria-expands-deep-tech-skills-pipeline/nWJadvbND1yvbjO1)).
3. **Café One is now a national network.** It went from 16 outlets in 8 states ([Condia, 4 Feb 2025](https://thecondia.com/cafe-one-raises-coworking-prices/)) to 35 hubs in 13 cities ([Zikoko partner content, 6 Nov 2026](https://www.zikoko.com/announcements/cafe-one-is-making-it-easier-to-do-your-best-work/)). Its own site lists 37 entries, 21 of them in Lagos, including UNILAG and LASU ([cafeone.ng, accessed 17 Nov 2026](https://cafeone.ng/locations)). Costs are its pain point: prices rose up to 37% in 2025 because of diesel and rent, and 7.5% VAT was added in Jan 2026 ([Condia, 8 Jan 2026](https://thecondia.com/cafe-one-2026-coworking-vat-price-update-nigeria/)). Pitch a co-branded "Café One Lab", not a rival café.
4. **Sterling's HEART agenda maps onto every lab zone, and Sterling has made no public statement about makerspaces, so the lab would be a first.**
   - The fit: health (wet lab, drones; Sterling already funds Zipline drone deliveries), education, agriculture, renewables and transport (EV/solar technicians) ([sterling.ng/heart](https://sterling.ng/heart/)).
   - At Café One's launch, then-ED Yemi Odubiyi, now Group MD of the holding company, called it "what the bank of the future looks like" ([ThisDay](https://thisdaylive.com/index.php/2019/03/10/sterling-bank-unveils-cafe-one-in-lekki)).
5. **Miva has no engineering degrees.** Its lab need is in Computing/AI/IT and in Allied Health (Nursing, Public Health, Community Health, Environmental Health) ([miva.edu.ng](https://miva.edu.ng/miva-programmes)).
   - NUC's ODL guidelines allow face-to-face "practicum" and say "collaboration between providers" is a cost-effective way to provide study centres ([NUC ODL Guidelines](https://nuc.edu.ng/wp-content/uploads/2015/01/GUIDELINES-FOR-OPEN-AND-DISTANCE-LEARNING-IN-NIGERIAN-UNIVERSITIES.pdf)).
   - Miva planned a second Lagos centre for Q1 2027 ([Miva, 2 May 2025](https://miva.edu.ng/?p=19055)).
6. **CcHUB is a programme partner, not a funder or a hardware peer.** Bosun Tijani left to become minister in Aug 2023, and Ojoma Ochai has been MD since Dec 2023 ([Techeconomy, 15 Dec 2023](https://techeconomy.ng/ojoma-ochai-takes-role-as-managing-director-of-co-creation-hub/)). Its focus is education, health and creative industries ([Techpoint, 10 Mar 2026](https://techpoint.africa/insight/cchub-wants-to-build-ecosystem-infrastructure/)). The only maker asset I found is a light American Corner makerspace. It is also adding private offices in Yaba ([Liners, 1 Jun 2026](https://liners.com/news/cchub-expands-yaba-lagos-hq-private-startup-offices)), so it is partly a competitor.
7. **The clearest federal door is open now: 3MTT's Delivery Network.** It has ₦4bn of outcome-based funding for Cohorts 5–6, and Cohort 5 applications close **30 Nov 2026** ([Nairametrics, 12 Nov 2026](https://nairametrics.com/2026/11/12/fg-opens-applications-for-3mtt-delivery-partners-commits-n4-billion-for-cohorts-5-6/)). iDICE (~$617M, run by BOI) is building 66 hubs at universities and polytechnics, so approach it with a host institution ([BOI, 17 Jun 2026](https://www.boi.ng/nigeria-has-a-plan-for-its-next-generation-of-founders-its-called-idice/)).

### Strongest pitch angle
**"Café One Lab: the first Café One where Nigerians build HEART hardware."**
- **Café One** gets a premium hub format with machine time it can sell through its existing Café One Card and All Access plans. Miva students already use Café One as their community centres.

- **Health** connects through the drone cage and wet lab, which echo the Zipline deliveries and Ilera Eko booths. These are showcase material for ASIS 2027.

### Possible deal structures
1. **Co-brand and operating partnership.** Café One runs the café and coworking floor, either as a revenue-share tenant or on its own P&L. The lab operator runs the machines and wet lab. Branding would be "Café One Lab".
2. **HEART financing.** Imperium-financed solar and batteries for the building, since power is Café One's stated cost pain, plus an equipment loan.
3. **CSR sponsorship** through Sterling One Foundation: lab passes or a named fellowship for Beyond Education scholars.
4. **Space.** Site the lab in a Sterling-owned or branch property; Café One already operates inside branches.
5. **Equity** is the least likely structure for a bank *(judgment; regulatory limits not checked)*.

### How open universities meet practical-lab requirements
- **NUC ODL Guidelines** (PDF uploaded 2015; its scope section refers to 2009–2015, and I found no newer edition) ([NUC](https://nuc.edu.ng/wp-content/uploads/2015/01/GUIDELINES-FOR-OPEN-AND-DISTANCE-LEARNING-IN-NIGERIAN-UNIVERSITIES.pdf)):
  - Students "should not be required to attend classes or have face-to-face contact, unless there are compelling reasons to justify it. Such as Examinations, periodic facilitation and practicum."
  - Study centres should "have agreed standards of accommodation in facilities and equipment."
  - "Collaboration between providers, e.g. in 'university centres' will offer a cost effective means of providing study centres."
  - Universities may offer ODL only in disciplines where they have the capability.
- **NOUN study-centre policy** (NOUN is the public ODL benchmark). Its study centres deliver "laboratory practicals" and SIWES (the national industrial-placement scheme), and a proposed centre must show it can provide a "laboratory for experimental work" ([NOUN policy PDF, Mar 2025](https://dqa.nou.edu.ng/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/NOUN-Policy-on-Establishment-of-Study-Centres.pdf)).
- **NOUN in practice:**
  - It runs zonal, in-person practicals in Physics, Chemistry, Biology and Computer Science, staffed by academics from other universities and a polytechnic. They are a graduation requirement and count for 15% of the tutor-marked assignment ([NOUN News, 5 Oct 2023](https://nounnews.nou.edu.ng/index.php/node/1227)).
