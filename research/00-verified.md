## Corrections to the working notes

- Café One has 20 hubs in Lagos. The 37 in earlier notes is the national total.
- Since 2025 the Café One discount applies to any Café One cardholder, not only Sterling Bank customers.
- Funding rounds for Nigerian hardware companies in 2026 are not used anywhere public, because they could not be checked well enough.
