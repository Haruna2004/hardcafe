# Lagos hardware coworking lab: economics & pricing inputs (research 02)

## Key takeaways

1. **FX and inflation baseline.** On 16 Nov 2026 the official NFEM rate was **₦1,353.97/US$**. The parallel market was ₦1,390–₦1,445, and the gap to the official rate was ₦86.50 on 12 Nov. At ₦1,355/$, the **$300k budget is about ₦406.5m**. Headline inflation was **14.06% y/y in Oct 2026**: food 19.13%, core 10.78%, and Lagos food 31.43%. ([withinnigeria](https://www.withinnigeria.com/2026/11/17/dollar-to-naira-today-nov-16-2026-cbn-rate-%E2%82%A61353-97-black-market-%E2%82%A61390/), [Nairametrics](https://nairametrics.com/2026/11/13/naira-depreciates-to-n1445-in-parallel-market-as-gap-with-official-rate-widens-to-n86-50/), [VON](https://von.gov.ng/nigerias-headline-inflation-falls-to-14-06-in-october/), [Vanguard](https://www.vanguardngr.com/2026/11/inflation-rate-drops-year-on-year-rises-month-on-month/))
2. **CafeOne sets the mass-market price, and it charges per pass. It is not "free with a Sterling account".** The 2026 rates include VAT. Non-cardholders pay **₦8,525/day, ₦42,625/week and ₦170,501/month**. Cardholders pay ₦6,310, ₦31,552 and ₦126,209. Student day passes start at ₦2,150. The month costs exactly 20 day passes, so there is no volume discount. CafeOne has **20 hubs in Lagos and 36 nationwide**, up from 16 outlets in Feb 2025. ([Condia, 8 Jan 2026](https://thecondia.com/cafe-one-2026-coworking-vat-price-update-nigeria/), [cafeone.ng/locations](https://www.cafeone.ng/locations))
3. **IWG sets the premium price.** On 16 Nov 2026 its open desks cost **₦176,900–₦300,900 per person per month** (about $131–$222), and private offices cost **₦337,000–₦779,800/month** ([workin.space](https://workin.space/en/nigeria/lagos)). On a per-working-day basis, an IWG monthly desk (₦8,041–₦13,677) costs about the same as a CafeOne day pass. The published rates for mid-market hubs (Leadspace, Venia, Workstation) date from around 2022, before the devaluation, and are not usable.
4. **Affordability is the binding constraint.** Median formal-sector gross pay was **₦217,000/month** in July 2026. The tech-sector median was ₦418,916 and the software-engineer median ₦1.07m ([PaidHR via TechCabal/EdgeX](https://pro.edgex.exchange/en-US/news/article/nigeria-salary-report-paidhr), [Employsome](https://employsome.com/blog/average-salary-nigeria/)). Graduate engineers earn about ₦150k–₦350k. The minimum wage is **₦70,000** federally and ₦85,000 for Lagos public servants. A month at CafeOne prices is **79% of median formal pay and 41% of the tech median**. Memberships therefore need to be priced at roughly ₦50k–₦130k or paid by employers, with machine time sold as credits.
5. **Asking rents (₦/m²/yr):**
   - Ikeja warehouses: ₦15k–₦85k (1,000 m² units list at ₦40m–₦50m a year).
   - Surulere warehouses: about ₦11.5k–₦62.5k.
   - Yaba offices and commercial space: ₦50k–₦127k.
   - Lekki Phase 1 offices: ₦100k–₦400k.
   - Victoria Island (VI) Grade A: $35–$60/m²/month (≈₦570k–₦976k/m²/yr).

   Service charges add roughly ₦15k–₦40k/m²/yr. (Sections 3.1–3.3.)
6. **Upfront rent is the biggest cash red flag.** Listings commonly ask for **2 years' rent in advance**, plus about **10% agency + 10% legal + 5–20% caution**. That is about **2.3× annual rent at move-in**. For 1,200 m² in Yaba at ₦70k/m², move-in is about **₦193m ($143k, 48% of the budget)**. A 1,570 m² Yaba building at ₦200m/yr with 2 years upfront needs ₦400m, which is the whole budget.
   - **Legal risk:** Section 4 of the Lagos Tenancy Law 2011 makes it an offence for a new tenant to pay, or a landlord to demand, more than 1 year in advance. The penalty is a ₦100k fine or 3 months. The law applies everywhere except Apapa, Ikeja GRA, Ikoyi and VI ([statute quoted](https://s3.amazonaws.com/documents.jdsupra.com/1e0873ca-430e-4db2-b136-92543387c681.pdf)).
   - A bill that would cap agency fees at 5% was still pending in May–Sept 2026 ([Pulse](https://www.pulse.ng/story/lagos-rent-bill-agent-fees-crackdown-2026052706520753843)).
7. **Power: grid, diesel and solar compared.**
   - **Grid:** Band A costs **₦209.50/kWh** (since 7 Dec 2025).
   - **Diesel generator:** **₦519–₦960/kWh**. Diesel was ₦1,688/L at Lagos depots and ₦1,730/L retail in Nov 2026. It peaked at ₦2,023.75 in Apr 2026 and was ₦940 a year earlier.
   - **Solar (commercial & industrial, C&I):** about **₦1.1m–₦1.5m per kWp** ($812–$1,107), with lithium (LFP) storage at **₦250k–₦380k per usable kWh** installed. A 50 kWp + 100 kWh system costs **₦80m–₦113m ($59k–$83k)**. That is too large for the capex budget unless it is leased or bought through a power purchase agreement (PPA). The last public PPA benchmark is ₦169/kWh, from 2024.

   ([billsguide](https://www.billsguide.ng/electricity-rates/ekedc), [Legit](https://www.legit.ng/business-economy/energy/1741139-petrol-prices-drop-lagos-depots-cut-rates-n1200-diesel-falls-n1688/), [GlobalPetrolPrices](https://www.globalpetrolprices.com/Nigeria/diesel_prices/), [MIMAH](https://mimah.net/posts/commercial-solar-installation-cost-nigeria))
8. **Internet.**
   - **Fibre:** ipNX's SME plans cost **₦30,525/month (35 Mbps) to ₦268,500/month (600 Mbps)**, plus ₦55,900–₦68,625 setup.
   - **Starlink Business (Priority):** **₦159,000/month** plus a ₦590,000 kit. The ₦57,000 residential plan is waitlisted in VI, Ikoyi, Lekki and Surulere.
   - **Budget:** about ₦290k/month for fibre with satellite backup.

   ([ipNX](https://ipnxnigeria.ng/sme-plans/), [Legit](https://www.legit.ng/business-economy/technology/1698748-starlink-unveils-n159000-priority-plan-lagos-abuja-residential-users-remain-locked-out/))
9. **Staff (monthly gross, from listings and guides):**
   - Makerspace or lab technician: ₦150k–₦300k.
   - Electronics engineer: ₦150k–₦600k.
   - Facility or lab manager: ₦150k–₦400k.
   - Community or operations manager: ₦250k–₦900k.
   - Barista: ₦100k–₦150k.
   - Cleaner or guard: about ₦70k–₦100k (the legal floor is ₦70k).

   Add about **12% statutory on-costs**: employer pension 10%, NSITF 1% and ITF 1%.
10. **Equipment list prices (USD, Nov 2026):**
    - Prusa CORE One+: **$1,203** assembled.
    - Formlabs Form 4 Basic: **$2,625**.
    - xTool P2S 55W: **$2,999**.
    - OMTech Pro 2440 100W: **$6,900**.
    - Makera Carvera: **$4,799**, or **$5,269** for the PCB bundle. Bantam Tools no longer lists a PCB mill.
    - Rigol DHO804: $459. Rigol DP832: $449. Hakko FX-888DX: $130.
    - Applied Biosystems MiniAmp PCR: $2,743 promotional / $4,220 list.

    **Not verified:** Bambu Lab (its store blocked fetches), xTool F1, a 1325 CNC router, LPKF, and the big lab items (biosafety cabinet, autoclave, CO₂ incubator, fume hood, lab fridge/freezer).
11. **Local parts cost more than US retail.** On Jumia (17 Nov 2026) an Arduino Uno R4 WiFi costs **₦60,000 ($44)**, 2.1× the $20.62 Arduino US store price. ESP32 boards cost ₦12k–₦20k. **PLA filament costs ₦50k–₦58k/kg ($37–$43)**. Material alone is about **₦50 per gram**, so per-gram print prices must sit well above that (estimate: ₦125–₦200/g).
12. **Import costs are only partly verified.**
    - **Verified:** VAT is 7.5%. Customs duty is 5–35% by HS code, charged on the CIF value (cost + insurance + freight) and payable in naira. Insurance must be placed with a Nigerian insurer, and all imports are inspected on arrival.
    - **Not verified:** HS-line rates; the status of the CISS, ETLS and 4%-of-FOB levies; clearing fees; lead times; authorised Lagos resellers; Computer Village prices; Lagos 3D-print service prices; prices for kids' STEM classes and IoT bootcamps.

    Get a licensed customs agent quote before finalising capex.

**How it charges**
- CafeOne sells pay-per-use passes priced per day. The week costs 5× the day rate and the month 20×, so longer passes carry no discount. (E: ₦8,525 × 20 = ₦170,500.)
- The membership card gives about a 26% discount: 1 − 6,310/8,525 = 25.98% (E).
- Since Jan 2025 the card's benefits have been open to **all cardholders, not only Sterling Bank customers** ([Condia 2025](https://thecondia.com/cafe-one-raises-coworking-prices/)). So it is not "free with an account".
- From Jan 2026 prices include 7.5% VAT, which CafeOne describes as a requirement of the new tax law ([Condia 2026](https://thecondia.com/cafe-one-2026-coworking-vat-price-update-nigeria/)).
- Meeting rooms and workstations are priced per hub, with "standard and wallet rates". These were not published in a form I could fetch ([cafeone.ng/meeting-rooms](https://www.cafeone.ng/meeting-rooms)).

**Locations** (per [cafeone.ng/locations](https://www.cafeone.ng/locations), accessed 17 Nov 2026; F)
- **20 Lagos hubs:** Adebola House (Opebi), Adeniran Ogunsanya (Surulere), Adeola Odeku (VI), Ajah, Aromire (Ikeja), Chevron (Lekki), Costain, Dopemu, Herbert Macaulay (Yaba; Sterling Bank building, 260 Herbert Macaulay Way), Ikate, Ikeja, Ikoyi, LASU (Ojo), Lekki, Marina, Ogudu, One Haven (Magodo), Oregun, Unilag (Akoka) and Yaba/Ozone (E-Centre, 1–11 Commercial Ave).
- There is also an Event Store in VI.
- **16 hubs in other states**, giving **36 hubs in total**. In Feb 2025 there were 16 outlets in 8 states ([Condia 2025](https://thecondia.com/cafe-one-raises-coworking-prices/)).
- **Implication (E):** CafeOne has two hubs in Yaba and three in Ikeja/Oregun/Opebi. Any café-coworking offer there competes head-on with ₦6,310–₦8,525/day.

### 1.4 What this means for pricing (E)
- An IWG monthly desk works out to ₦176,900/22 = **₦8,041** up to ₦300,900/22 = **₦13,677** per working day. Regulars at the premium end therefore pay about a CafeOne day pass per day, plus a 24-month contract (per IWG pages).
- The mid-market (₦50k–₦130k/month) is thinly documented in current sources. That is the band the affordability maths in section 2 points to.

### 3.4 Lease norms and the law

**Lagos Tenancy Law 2011** (statutory text quoted in a 2013 lawyer's commentary: [JDSupra PDF](https://s3.amazonaws.com/documents.jdsupra.com/1e0873ca-430e-4db2-b136-92543387c681.pdf); F)
- **Scope (s.1):** applies "to all premises within Lagos State, including business and residential premises". **Apapa, Ikeja GRA, Ikoyi and Victoria Island are exempt.**
- **s.4(3):** it is unlawful for a landlord to demand or receive more than **1 year's** rent from a would-be tenant.
- **s.4(4):** it is unlawful for a new or would-be tenant to **offer or pay** more than 1 year.
- **s.4(5):** the penalty is a **₦100,000 fine or 3 months' imprisonment**.
- **Implication:** paying 2 years upfront in Yaba, Surulere or Ikeja outside the GRA exposes the tenant as well as the landlord. A bank financing the lease may care about this.

**Market practice**
- Listings still ask for multi-year advances. Examples: "Minimum of 2 years' advance rent" on the 1,570 m² Yaba building; "5 years lease term with minimum of 2 years payment upfront" ([NPC Yaba commercial](https://nigeriapropertycentre.com/commercial/lagos/yaba/showtype); S).

**Fees**
- **Agency 10% + legal 10%** of annual rent is the common norm ([uni2study](https://www.uni2study.com/articles/agency-caution-legal-fees-renting-nigeria); S).
- A LASRERA (Lagos real-estate regulator) provision reportedly recognises **15%** agency where several agents are involved ([PropertyPro blog](https://propertypro.ng/blog/?p=13561); S).
- **Caution deposit:** 5–20% ([uni2study](https://www.uni2study.com/articles/agency-caution-legal-fees-renting-nigeria), [Nairametrics](https://nairametrics.com/?p=411650); S).

**Pending reform**
- The Lagos Tenancy and Recovery of Premises Bill would:
  - cap agent commission at **5%**;
  - ban more than 1 year's advance rent for new tenants and more than 3 months for existing tenants;
  - **apply statewide, removing the exemptions**.
- Status: at committee stage and unsigned on 27 May 2026 ([Pulse](https://www.pulse.ng/story/lagos-rent-bill-agent-fees-crackdown-2026052706520753843); F), and still a proposal in Sept 2026 ([withinnigeria](https://www.withinnigeria.com/2026/09/09/new-lagos-tenancy-rules-what-the-bill-says-about-3-month-rent-5-agency-fees-and-illegal-eviction/); S).

These need a follow-up search or mystery-shopping calls to 5–10 providers.

### 9.3 Cost-based price floors (E): use until benchmarks are collected
- **FDM printing:**
  - Material cost = ₦50,000/kg ÷ 1,000 = **₦50/g**, from Jumia PLA.
  - At 2.5×, 3× and 4× material cost (my markup assumption, covering waste, machine time, labour and margin): **₦125 / ₦150 / ₦200 per gram**.
  - That is $0.09–$0.15/g at ₦1,355.
- **Capital recovery per machine-hour:** landed capex ÷ (useful life in years × billable hours per year). Example: OMTech 100W at $6,900 FOB, over 3 years at 1,000 billable hours a year = **$2.30/hour (≈₦3,117/hour)**. This excludes import uplift, power, consumables and staff, so it is a floor, not a price.

## Gaps, caveats and red flags

**Red flags for the bank case**
- **Upfront rent:** 2 years' rent plus fees is about 2.3× annual rent, which takes 25–48% of the $300k for a Yaba/Ikeja/Surulere site and more than the whole budget in Lekki or VI (section 3.5). Paying more than 1 year upfront is also an offence for the tenant under s.4 of the 2011 Tenancy Law, outside the exempt areas.
- **Diesel:** prices swung from ₦940 to ₦2,023.75 to ₦1,730 per litre within 12 months. Genset power costs 2.5–4.6× Band A, and Band A supply shortfalls led to a NERC compensation order in 2026.
- **Starlink:** residential plans are waitlisted in VI, Ikoyi, Lekki and Surulere, so plan on the ₦159k/month business tier.
- **FX:** USD capex is exposed to FX moves. The parallel market sat about 6% above the official rate in mid-Nov 2026.
- **Affordability:** a CafeOne-level monthly price is 79% of median formal-sector pay. Revenue will lean on day passes, corporate or university sponsorship, and machine credits.

**Data quality**
- Many rent and salary figures are **asking prices or postings read from search summaries (S)**, because the main property and job portals (NPC, PropertyPro, PrivateProperty, Indeed) blocked direct fetches with HTTP 403.
- Mid-market coworking rate cards (Leadspace, Venia, Workstation, CcHUB) are **stale (about 2022)**.

**Not covered: follow-up needed**
- HS-specific duties and levies, clearing costs, lead times.
- Bambu Lab prices; xTool F1; 1325 CNC router; LPKF.
- Large lab equipment: biosafety cabinet, fume hood, autoclave, CO₂ incubator, benchtop centrifuge, fridge/freezer.
- Lagos resellers and Computer Village prices; AliExpress comparisons.
- Global makerspace machine-time rates; Lagos 3D-print service per-gram prices.
- Lagos kids' STEM/robotics class fees and Nigerian IoT bootcamp fees.

**Why these are missing**
- The shared web-search budget (200 per turn) ran out.
- workstation.ng, leadspace.ng, artisansasylum.org, gearbox.co.ke and trade.gov.ng did not resolve from this session. They may need to be allowed in the session's network settings.
