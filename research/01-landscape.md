# 01 - Landscape and comparables: Lagos hardware coworking lab

## Key takeaways

1. **I found nobody building this in Lagos.** No public source shows a café-coworking space that also has a makerspace, a components store and a shared wet lab, open or announced, in Lagos or anywhere in Nigeria. The Fab Foundation directory lists 11 Nigerian labs. Only 1 is active in Lagos (UNILAG's "mini fab lab") and 1 more is "planned" (Afconrecruit, Ojodu Berger). 6 of the 11 are in the FCT. ([fablabs.io API](https://api.fablabs.io/0/labs.json), pulled 2026-11-17)
2. **What Lagos has today is fragmented and partial:**
   - CcHUB's free, education-grade American Corner makerspace in Yaba (3D printers, Raspberry Pi, LEGO; weekdays 10am-5pm). ([CcHUB](https://cchubnigeria.com/americancorner/))
   - The French-donated UNILAG FabLab (2020). In March 2026 its own training event named "Dusty Machine Syndrome", filament and acrylic supply problems, and ageing equipment with scarce spares. ([UNILAG, 2026-03-03](https://unilag.edu.ng/?p=70562))
   - The art-led 16/16 Makerspace, which opened in August 2026 with one Bambu Lab printer. ([Moonlighting, 2026-08-21](https://journal.moonlight.ng/p/makerspace-aug-2026))
   - A 25 m² Siemens Stiftung training makerspace (March 2025). ([Siemens Stiftung](https://www.siemens-stiftung.org/en/media/news/new-makerspace-inaugurated-in-lagos-to-enhance-career-prospects-for-nigerian-youth/))
   - Arridex's industrial additive-manufacturing Omnifactory (commissioned June 2026). It is B2B, not open access. ([Nigeria Housing Market](https://www.nigeriahousingmarket.com/news/lagos-commissions-arridex-omnifactory-west-africas-first-additive-manufacturing-facility))
3. **Public money for fab labs is going to Abuja and inland hubs, not Lagos:**
   - NITDA's NCAIR FabLab (Abuja, May 2022). ([Techeconomy](https://techeconomy.ng/nitda-commission-abuja-fabrication-laboratory-fablab-1-0-at-ncair-photos-story/))
   - JICA's $10.4M Renewed Hope Startup Hub with digital fabrication (Wuye, Abuja; foundation laid 23 Oct 2026). ([NHM](https://www.nigeriahousingmarket.com/news/japan-10-4m-nigeria-startup-innovation-hub))
   - The 13,000 m² Ilorin Innovation Hub, with UNDP as makerspace anchor tenant (operating since early 2025). ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ilorin_Innovation_Hub))
   - Lagos's own instruments fund startups and research, not shared labs: the LASRIC ₦1bn seed fund (2024) and the draft ₦31bn innovation fund (2025). ([The Condia](https://thecondia.com/lagos-1billion-startup-funding-innovation-research/), [TechCabal](https://techcabal.com/2025/08/20/lagos-31bn-startup-innovation-fund/))
4. **No shared or community wet lab exists in Lagos as far as I could find.** The only Nigerian open-biolab attempt I found (Abuja area, equipment shipped 2016) has unknown status. ([Biomaker, search summary](https://www.biomaker.org/blog/2017/4/21/setting-up-an-open-synthetic-biology-lab-in-abuja-nigeria)) The closest working African model is Hive Biolab at Kumasi Hive in Ghana (2019), which ran a wet-lab fellowship cohort in October 2026. ([DIYbio Sphere](https://sphere.diybio.org/labs/hivebiolab/), [X, search summary](https://x.com/kumasihive))
5. **Gearbox (Nairobi) is the closest African analog, and a warning:**
   - A 20,000 sq ft makerspace priced at $40-$100/month. ([Maitri Capital case study, 2022](https://www.maitricapital.com/static/media/Gearbox%20-%20A_Case_Study_for_Understanding_Kenyan_Startup_Ecosystem.f88bab5c57cff3f93a29.pdf))
   - It raised about $5M (Sh725M) over 9 years, with about 90% from one funder (Lemelson).
   - When that money ended in 2022, it laid off about three-quarters of its permanent staff and handed back half its space (2023). ([Business Daily, 2023-08-30](https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/lifestyle/personal-finance/how-funds-drought-slowed-nairobi-s-innovation-beacon--4353054))
   - What survived is a for-profit PCB line, GbE Circuits: about 100 clients, of which 10 international clients bring about 80% of revenue, and "edging toward breakeven". ([Business Daily, 2025-07-09](https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/corporate/enterprise/gbe-circuits-reboots-kenya-electronics-hardware-dream-5112042))
6. **TechShop showed that memberships alone can't carry big-box makerspaces.**
   - It had 10 US sites and 9,000+ members, charged $125/month (2014), spent $2.5-3.5M to open each shop, and targeted 500 members per site. ([Make:, 2014](https://makezine.com/article/maker-news/techshops-not-so-secret-ingredient/))
   - It closed and announced Chapter 7 liquidation in November 2017. CEO Dan Woods: "A for-profit network of wholly owned makerspaces is impossible to sustain without outside subsidy." ([Make:, 2017-11-15](https://makezine.com/article/maker-news/techshop-closes-doors-files-bankruptcy/))
   - Our ~$300k budget is about 9-12% of what one TechShop cost to open.
7. **Café plus fabrication works as a brand and community engine, not a fabrication business.**
   - FabCafe started in Tokyo in 2012 and claims 13 locations in 2026. ([Loftwork](https://loftwork.com/en/where/fabcafe-global/))
   - It sells corporate co-creation programmes priced from about $9.3k to $47k. ([FabCafe](https://fabcafe.com/business/))
   - Its Tokyo COO said in 2023 that fewer people now visit "for the purpose of digital fabrication itself". ([Loftwork, 2023-03-01](https://loftwork.com/en/finding/loftwork_is_07/))
   - The newest FabCafe (Chiba, June 2026) lists no fabrication machines. ([Loftwork](https://loftwork.com/en/news/2026/05/20_fabcafe-chiba/))
8. **Bank cafés are marketing assets with undisclosed P&Ls.**
   - Capital One runs 65 cafés (December 2025; first opened 2014) and won't disclose their costs or revenue. ([American Banker, 2025-12-30](https://www.americanbanker.com/news/from-cafes-to-pokemon-go-banks-rethink-physical-spaces), [Marketing Brew, 2025-06-27](https://www.marketingbrew.com/stories/2025/06/27/brand-coffee-shops-uniqlo-capital-one))
   - Sterling's Café One site lists 37 locations across 12 states and the FCT, 21 of them in Lagos (one is an "Event Store"). They include **UNILAG (Akoka)** and **Herbert Macauley (Yaba)**. ([cafeone.ng](https://cafeone.ng/locations), fetched 2026-11-17; an older [BusinessDay piece](https://clone.businessday.ng/?p=1026463) cited "22 hubs across 9 states", per a search summary)
   - 2026 prices: a day pass is ₦8,525 (₦6,310 for cardholders) and a month is ₦170,501 (₦126,209). ([The Condia, 2026-01-08](https://thecondia.com/cafe-one-2026-coworking-vat-price-update-nigeria/))
9. **Member-funded US makerspaces earn most of their own revenue, but margins are thin or negative.** Where dues are booked as program revenue, the earned share is 63-98% (IRS Form 990 data via [ProPublica](https://projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/)):
   - Maker Nexus (28,000 sq ft, $160/month) earned 88% of its $1.58M revenue in 2023, with payroll at 59% of costs.
   - Dallas Makerspace earned 98% of $0.84M with $0 payroll, and still ran a $108k deficit.
   - Artisan's Asylum lost $0.85M in FY2023, with liabilities ($13.1M) above assets ($12.4M).
10. **Sizing benchmarks:**
    - A standard fab lab is about $120k of equipment in about 140 m², staffed by about 5-6 people over two shifts. ([Fab Foundation](https://fabfoundation.org/getting-started/))
    - The Fab Foundation's sample budget for a "very high end lab in a western city" is $398,583 to start plus $289,433 a year. In it, laser and CNC-router upkeep is about 8% of purchase price per year, and about 17% of all hardware capex once the repairs and replacement lines are added (my calculation, §4.6). ([sample budget](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/e/2PACX-1vSOldh7H1KLJWCjBx--nzrBqBWyBcVFyB7XCxGFBqbNCqNMvejxdFD9zg_tmlog6HKBmmhql3dRTp5K/pubhtml/sheet?headers=false&gid=341525378))
    - UK survey: the average makerspace is 209 m², 60% have 50 or fewer members, and 42% are staffed by volunteers. ([Nesta, 2015](https://www.nesta.org.uk/report/open-dataset-of-uk-makerspaces-a-users-guide/))
11. **Shared-lab businesses need dense biotech demand.**
    - BioCurious charges $130/month plus $500/month per 6-ft bench, yet 97% of its 2023 revenue was booked as contributions. ([BioCurious](https://biocurious.org/membership), [ProPublica](https://projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/organizations/273848068))
    - Genspace ran a $261k deficit on $481k revenue in 2023. ([ProPublica](https://projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/organizations/270947943))
    - LabCentral's residents and alumni raised $1.25B in 2025 alone. ([LabCentral](https://www.labcentral.org/2025-impact-report)) BioLabs has 21 sites and 500+ companies. ([BioLabs](https://www.biolabs.io/))
12. **Data gap:** I found no credible published benchmark for makerspace membership churn or machine-hour utilization. Treat any such figures in the deck as assumptions to test in a pilot.

## Scope, method and limits

Prepared 17 Nov 2026. Scope: who already runs (or ran, or plans) makerspaces, fab labs, hardware hubs and shared wet labs in Lagos/Nigeria; African and global comparables; published operating stats.

Method: web search plus direct fetches of primary pages. I also pulled the Fab Foundation lab directory (https://api.fablabs.io/0/labs.json, retrieved 17 Nov 2026, 2,898 entries) and IRS Form 990 data from the ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer API. Three limits apply:
- The shared web-search budget ran out partway through. I could not run every Lagos sweep: university labs at LASU, YABATECH, Covenant and Pan-Atlantic; Bank of Industry; private studios in Lekki/VI; component retailers.
- Several sites blocked automated fetches: TechCabal, Technext, Sweetcrude, TheCable, Science Nigeria, Western Post, Medium, Kickstarter, allAfrica. Facts taken only from search-result summaries are marked "(search summary)".
- "Not found" in this document means "not found in public sources as of 17 Nov 2026", not proof of absence.

### 1.5 Programmes and funders named in the brief

- **LASRIC (Lagos State Science, Research & Innovation Council)**
  - Reconstituted 15 June 2024 with a ₦1bn (~$680k) fund for seed grants, research and school STEM programmes; 20+ startups funded so far. No lab or makerspace funding is mentioned. ([The Condia, 2024-06-17](https://thecondia.com/lagos-1billion-startup-funding-innovation-research/))
  - The earlier council secured the state's first patent, for a ventilator designed by a UNILAG team. This is a hardware precedent. ([IT Pulse, 2024-06-18](https://itpulse.com.ng/?p=21802))
- **NITDA**: runs the NCAIR FabLab and has the hubs-per-state target (above).
- **3MTT**: phase 1 had 30,000 fellows (from Dec 2023); phase 2 targets 270,000. All listed skills are software, data, design or security. No hardware track and no fab labs are mentioned. ([3MTT](https://3mtt.nitda.gov.ng/))
- **CcHUB**
  - The American Corner makerspace (above) and a June 2026 office expansion. No hardware lab found.
  - It reported deploying $4.18M in grants in 2025. ([TechCabal 2026-03-10, search summary](https://techcabal.com/2026/03/10/cchub-deployed-4-18-million-in-grants-in-2025/))
  - Note: CcHUB acquired Nairobi's iHub. ([TechCrunch, search result](https://techcrunch.com/?p=1880482)) iHub's funding set up Gearbox. ([Maitri case study](https://www.maitricapital.com/static/media/Gearbox%20-%20A_Case_Study_for_Understanding_Kenyan_Startup_Ecosystem.f88bab5c57cff3f93a29.pdf)) CcHUB's own network therefore holds the Gearbox lessons.

- **Development partners active in Nigerian labs**: Embassy of France (5 university fab labs in 2020, Nile University in 2024, FabLab management training in 2026); JICA; UNDP; Siemens Stiftung/BMZ; NASENI and AfriLabs (₦250M InnovateNaija challenge, opened 4 Sep 2025). ([UNILAG 2026](https://unilag.edu.ng/?p=70562), [BusinessDay (search summary)](https://businessday.ng/technology/article/naseni-unveils-n250m-youth-innovation-challenge-to-attract-solutions/))

### 1.6 Demand signals and pain points

- **Components are the main bottleneck.** A 2018 Hardware Lagos survey: "Supply chain, the ease of getting components and materials, is the single biggest headache of makers in Nigeria. Funding follows quite closely." ([Medium, search summary; page blocked to fetch](https://medium.com/hardware-lagos/makers-by-the-numbers-ec5502a52395)) In Kenya, GbE's CTO: "Startups used to wait weeks for their PCBs to arrive from China or Europe." ([Business Daily 2025](https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/corporate/enterprise/gbe-circuits-reboots-kenya-electronics-hardware-dream-5112042))
- **Programme graduates have nowhere to keep building.** GE Garage participants "leave when it ends" with no local makerspace to keep refining products. Its curriculum had no electronics. ([Make: 2018](https://makezine.com/article/maker-news/access-sustainability-makerspaces-new-york-nigeria/))
- **Hardware employers exist, but they manufacture outside Lagos.**
  - Terra Industries opened a 15,000 sq ft factory in Idu, Abuja in Feb 2025. ([Semafor, search summary](https://www.semafor.com/article/03/02/2026/the-drone-making-startup-with-big-dreams-of-securing-nigeria))
  - It raised $18M in Aug 2026 (total seed $52M). Its second "Pax" factory, 34,000 sq ft, is going to **Ghana** (Q4 2026). ([Techeconomy, 2026-08-17](https://techeconomy.ng/terra-industries-18m-africa-drone-factory))
- **Lagos now has industrial additive manufacturing (Arridex)** for parts a makerspace can't make. It is a partner, not a competitor.

## 3. Global comparables and lessons

### 3.1 TechShop (2006-2017)
- **Scale:** First site in Menlo Park in 2006. By 2017 it had 10 US and 4 licensed international sites (Abu Dhabi, Lille, Paris, Tokyo) and 9,000+ US members. ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TechShop), [Associations Now](https://associationsnow.com/2017/11/lessons-shutdown-techshops-machines-sputtered/))
- **Economics (2014):** $125/month; target of about 500 paying members per shop ("magic... once you hit 500 members"); $2.5-3.5M to open each shop; 18,000 sq ft San Francisco flagship; 100-200 classes a month. Partners such as Ford and Lowe's bought employee memberships; DARPA and the VA backed Arlington. ([Make: 2014](https://makezine.com/article/maker-news/techshops-not-so-secret-ingredient/))
- **Failure:** Closed all US sites on 15 Nov 2017 and announced Chapter 7 liquidation. CEO Dan Woods:
  - "operating on exceedingly low cash balances for quite some time"
  - "invested too many years and too many dollars trying to prop up the wrong business model"
  - "A for-profit network of wholly owned makerspaces is impossible to sustain without outside subsidy... readily available to non-profits, and very rarely an option for for-profit enterprises."
  ([Make: 2017](https://makezine.com/article/maker-news/techshop-closes-doors-files-bankruptcy/))
- It was delaying vendor payments near the end. It offered "$1.4 million in tools and machinery for... less than $4 per day". ([Associations Now](https://associationsnow.com/2017/11/lessons-shutdown-techshops-machines-sputtered/)) Its last-minute pivot to consulting ("makerspace midwife") came too late. ([IEEE Spectrum, 2017-11-16](https://spectrum.ieee.org/techshop-goes-bankrupt))
- **Aftermath:** The licensed international sites carried on (Tokyo closed Feb 2020). The TechShop 2.0 / TheShop.build successor shut in 2020 because of litigation costs. Ex-members founded nonprofits Protohaven (2018) and Maker Nexus (2019). ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TechShop))
- **Lessons:** Very high capex per site, owned sites, a for-profit structure with no access to grants, and fast expansion ended in a cash crunch. The licensed sites outlived the owner.

### 3.2 FabCafe (Loftwork, Tokyo 2012-)
- **Network:** Loftwork says FabCafe "now serves... 13 locations around the world"; the page names 11 (Tokyo, Kyoto, Osaka, Hida, Fuji, Taipei, Bangkok, Toulouse, Strasbourg, Mexico City, Kuala Lumpur). ([Loftwork](https://loftwork.com/en/where/fabcafe-global/)) Hong Kong left its Sheung Wan space in May 2021. ([FabCafe HK (search summary)](https://fabcafe.com/magazine/hk/announcement-fabcafe-hk-has-moved-out-of-the-space-in-sheung-wan-as-of-may-2021)) Chiba opened in June 2026 inside a public park teahouse. It lists café, local products, exhibitions and workshops, and no machines. ([Loftwork 2026](https://loftwork.com/en/news/2026/05/20_fabcafe-chiba/))
- **Money:** The business page (c. 2020) prices corporate programmes: hackathons from ¥1M (~$9,300); community co-creation and the MTRL materials-innovation programme from ¥3M (~$28,000); YouFab award sponsorship and global-network collaborations from ¥5M (~$47,000). It says the café gets more than 5,000 visitors a month. The legal entity is FabCafe LLP (Loftwork + Toshiya Fukuda). ([FabCafe](https://fabcafe.com/business/))
- **Trend:** Daiki Kanaoka (COO/CTO, FabCafe Tokyo) said fewer people visit "for the purpose of digital fabrication itself". The café's value is as "a place that amplifies individual interests" and a "testing ground". ([Loftwork, 2023-03-01](https://loftwork.com/en/finding/loftwork_is_07/))
- **Lesson:** The machines draw people in at first; the lasting revenue comes from the café, events and corporate projects. Plan for fabrication novelty to fade.

### 3.3 The Fab Lab network model
- **Size:** The Fab Foundation page says 1,750 labs in more than 100 countries. The live directory lists 2,898 entries: 2,030 active, 267 planned, 106 closed, 22 flagged "corona", 473 with no status. Self-reported statuses understate closures. ([Fab Foundation](https://fabfoundation.org/getting-started/), [fablabs.io API](https://api.fablabs.io/0/labs.json), pulled 2026-11-17)
- **Charter:** labs must be "open to the public for free or in-kind service/barter at least part of the time each week", share a common tool set and take part in the network. ([Fab Foundation](https://fabfoundation.org/getting-started/))
- **Cost and space:** about $120k average equipment for a standard lab (excluding transport, installation and training). The same page also gives $25-65k capital equipment plus $15-40k consumables. One of each machine needs about 1,500 sq ft (≈140 m²). The Foundation does not fund labs. ([Fab Foundation](https://fabfoundation.org/getting-started/))
- **Recommended revenue:** Fab Academy fees, workshops, usage fees or subscriptions, product sales or fabrication for others, host or donor funding, public-private partnerships. ([Fab Foundation](https://fabfoundation.org/getting-started/))
- **Two 2011 data points:**
  - Fab Lab Barcelona: about €9,000/month to run (rent-free); usage revenue only €50-500/week, the rest carried by its host institute IAAC.
  - Fab Lab Vestmannaeyjar (Iceland): €75,887/year, grant- and municipality-funded.
  ([Fab business wiki](https://wiki.fablab.is/index.php/wiki/Fab_business))

### 3.7 Lab coworking / lab-as-a-service
- **LabCentral** (Cambridge, MA; opened 2013; 501(c)(3)): 5 sites incl. Bayer Co.Lab and the Harvard Life Labs. "Home to 100 teams". Flexible month-to-month agreements; sponsor-funded "Golden Tickets". 2025: residents and alumni raised $1.25B ($21.85B since 2013) and created 7,915 jobs since 2013. Prices not published. ([LabCentral](https://www.labcentral.org/), [2025 impact report](https://www.labcentral.org/2025-impact-report))
- **BioLabs:** 21 sites (US, Europe, Japan, Canada); 500+ companies; members raised $5B+; 1,200+ jobs; 85+ Golden Tickets since 2018.
  - Fees are a per-person monthly membership plus a per-bench charge (or private lab). Shared equipment (PCR, HPLC, flow cytometry, autoclave), EHS, hazardous-waste removal and receiving are included.
  - Prices are set by site and not published.
  ([BioLabs](https://www.biolabs.io/), [features & fees](https://www.biolabs.io/featuresandfees))
- **Lesson:** These sell regulated, expensive lab infrastructure to VC-funded biotech teams, with corporate sponsors underwriting benches. That demand base isn't evident in Lagos. The copyable pieces are sponsor-funded benches and bundled EHS and waste handling.

## 4. Operating stats from surveys and literature

### 4.1 Revenue mix
- **UK (Nesta, 2015; 97 makerspaces):**
  - Turnover ranged from £0 to £350,000 (n=21).
  - 55% charge a flat fee, 24% charge none, 21% charge varied fees.
  - Income sources include membership, training, space hire, shops, cafés, grants, public funds and sponsorship. No mix percentages are given.
  - "Income from membership and other services isn't always guaranteed to cover costs... many makerspaces are often reliant on external support, such as grant funding or university subsidies."
  ([Nesta](https://www.nesta.org.uk/report/open-dataset-of-uk-makerspaces-a-users-guide/), [PDF](https://www.nesta.org.uk/documents/414/open_dataset_of_uk_makerspaces_users_guide.pdf))
- **US member makerspaces:** earned share 63-98% (Maker Nexus 88%, Artisan's 72%, Protohaven 63%, Dallas 98%), yet 3 of the 5 ran deficits in their latest filed year (table §3.4).
- **Fab labs:** Fab Lab Barcelona usage fees were €50-500/week against roughly €9k/month of costs; its host covered the gap (2011). ([wiki](https://wiki.fablab.is/index.php/wiki/Fab_business))
- **Café-led fabrication (FabCafe):** café, events and corporate programmes (¥1M-¥5M+); fabrication is not the core earner. ([FabCafe](https://fabcafe.com/business/), [Loftwork 2023](https://loftwork.com/en/finding/loftwork_is_07/))
- **Africa (Gearbox):** about 90% donor-funded until 2022. The survivor earns from PCB services, ~80% from 10 international clients. ([BD 2023](https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/lifestyle/personal-finance/how-funds-drought-slowed-nairobi-s-innovation-beacon--4353054), [BD 2025](https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/corporate/enterprise/gbe-circuits-reboots-kenya-electronics-hardware-dream-5112042))

### 4.3 Membership churn
**No reliable published benchmark found.** None of the sources I could access (Nesta, Fab Foundation, the 990s, TechShop coverage) report churn or retention. Qualitative signals:
- Founder burnout and passive members ("a tendency for the members to look to the founders to organise everything"). ([Nesta](https://www.nesta.org.uk/documents/414/open_dataset_of_uk_makerspaces_users_guide.pdf))
- TechShop's view that community reaches "critical mass" at about 500 members. ([Make: 2014](https://makezine.com/article/maker-news/techshops-not-so-secret-ingredient/))

Recommendation: measure monthly logo churn and cohort retention from the pilot. Do not cite an external figure.

### 4.4 Machine utilization
**No machine-hour utilization benchmark found.** Proxies:
- Fab Lab Vestmannaeyjar: 200-300 visits/week including students; 8-10 non-student users/day. Fab Lab Barcelona: about 60 people in a week (2011). ([wiki](https://wiki.fablab.is/index.php/wiki/Fab_business))
- UK: three-quarters of makerspaces had ≤250 unique visits in November 2014. ([Nesta](https://www.nesta.org.uk/report/open-dataset-of-uk-makerspaces-a-users-guide/))
- UNILAG FabLab (2026): "Dusty Machine Syndrome" (equipment underused because of limited project-based learning and weak user engagement). ([UNILAG 2026](https://unilag.edu.ng/?p=70562))
- FabCafe: more than 5,000 café visitors a month, but declining fabrication-driven visits. ([FabCafe](https://fabcafe.com/business/), [Loftwork 2023](https://loftwork.com/en/finding/loftwork_is_07/))

Machine supervision and troubleshooting during opening hours is the binding constraint.

### 4.6 Maintenance as % of capex
Neither the Fab Foundation nor Nesta publishes a ratio. The Fab Foundation sample budget lets one be derived (my math, from [the sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/e/2PACX-1vSOldh7H1KLJWCjBx--nzrBqBWyBcVFyB7XCxGFBqbNCqNMvejxdFD9zg_tmlog6HKBmmhql3dRTp5K/pubhtml/sheet?headers=false&gid=341525378)):

| Basis | Capex | Recurring per year | Ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| Epilog laser | $20,000 | $1,600 | 8.0% |
| ShopBot CNC router | $19,000 | $1,500 | 7.9% |
| Core machines (laser, router, spindle, vinyl, SRM-20) with the "Annual Repairs" line | $48,785 | $10,100 ($1,600 + $1,500 + $7,000) | 20.7% |
| All hardware lines (machines, computers, bench instruments, tools, AV, network) with repairs and replacements | $75,828 | $13,178 | 17.4% |

- Consumables and materials come on top. In that sheet the electronics, machine and fab materials total about $27k a year ($26,955 in Year 2), plus $13k of office consumables and print cartridges.
- Fab Lab Barcelona put about $1,000/month into "infrastructure, mostly replacement parts" (2011). ([wiki](https://wiki.fablab.is/index.php/wiki/Fab_business))
- **Nigeria-specific risk:** UNILAG reports filament and acrylic supply problems and "aging equipment with limited spare parts". ([UNILAG 2026](https://unilag.edu.ng/?p=70562))
- Estimate: budget 8-10% of machine capex per year for upkeep, plus a spares inventory, with 15-20% as the conservative envelope.

### 4.7 Common causes of failure (with evidence)
1. **Subsidy or single-donor dependence.** Gearbox had about 90% from Lemelson ([BD 2023](https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/lifestyle/personal-finance/how-funds-drought-slowed-nairobi-s-innovation-beacon--4353054)). TechShop said for-profits can't get the subsidies nonprofits receive ([Make: 2017](https://makezine.com/article/maker-news/techshop-closes-doors-files-bankruptcy/)). In the UK, grant or university reliance is common ([Nesta](https://www.nesta.org.uk/documents/414/open_dataset_of_uk_makerspaces_users_guide.pdf)).
2. **Capital intensity and fast roll-out.** TechShop spent $2.5-3.5M per shop across 10 owned sites and ended on low cash with vendor delays ([Make: 2014](https://makezine.com/article/maker-news/techshops-not-so-secret-ingredient/), [Associations Now](https://associationsnow.com/2017/11/lessons-shutdown-techshops-machines-sputtered/)).
3. **Real-estate exposure.** Artisan's Asylum ended FY2023 with liabilities above assets after its campus move ([990](https://projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/organizations/272604571)). Nova Labs carries a $3.8M secured mortgage ([990](https://projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/organizations/453796580)). Gearbox handed back half its space ([BD 2023](https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/lifestyle/personal-finance/how-funds-drought-slowed-nairobi-s-innovation-beacon--4353054)).
4. **Closed or programme-only access, so no community forms.** GE Lagos Garage was publicly open for one month in four years and declined community events ([Make: 2018](https://makezine.com/article/maker-news/access-sustainability-makerspaces-new-york-nigeria/)).
5. **Under-use and consumables or spares supply.** UNILAG's "Dusty Machine Syndrome" ([UNILAG 2026](https://unilag.edu.ng/?p=70562)).
6. **Governance and founder burnout.** Consensus decision-making "rubbish for getting to any decision"; "I had hoped it would run itself a lot more than it has" ([Nesta](https://www.nesta.org.uk/documents/414/open_dataset_of_uk_makerspaces_users_guide.pdf)).
7. **Fabrication novelty fading.** FabCafe ([Loftwork 2023](https://loftwork.com/en/finding/loftwork_is_07/)).
8. **Downsizing liabilities.** At Gearbox, severance and poor HR advice drained cash ([BD 2023](https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/lifestyle/personal-finance/how-funds-drought-slowed-nairobi-s-innovation-beacon--4353054), [BD 2025](https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/corporate/enterprise/gbe-circuits-reboots-kenya-electronics-hardware-dream-5112042)).

## 5. Pitch implications

### 5.1 The gap we fill (supported by §1)
- **Lagos has no paid, open-hours, professional-grade hardware floor.** Today it has free education kit (CcHUB American Corner), an under-used university lab (UNILAG), an art studio (16/16) and industrial B2B (Arridex). We sit between them: a lab anyone can join, with electronics benches, longer hours and staff support.
- **Components are the #1 pain point** (Hardware Lagos 2018; GbE quote). An on-site components store and PCB-ordering desk is a differentiator no Lagos space offers, as far as I found. Lagos component suppliers were not researched; verify before claiming exclusivity.
- **There is no shared wet lab in Lagos.** A small BSL-1 teaching and prototyping bench would be a genuine first, but the evidence says keep it small and sponsor-funded (§3.6-3.7).
- **The location writes itself:** Café One already has UNILAG and Herbert Macauley (Yaba) hubs. CcHUB is at 294 Herbert Macaulay Way. The UNILAG FabLab is on the Akoka campus. A Yaba/Akoka site plugs into all three.

### 5.2 Angles by target (from landscape evidence only)
- **Sterling / Café One.** Frame the lab as a flagship "Café One Lab": a brand and customer-acquisition asset, as Capital One frames its cafés. Show KPIs Sterling can track (sign-ups, cardholder usage, student traffic), not standalone fab profitability. Use Café One price points (₦8,525 day / ₦170,501 month, with cardholder discounts) as anchors.
- **CcHUB.** CcHUB is adding offices, not labs, and its iHub network already lived through Gearbox. Offer to be its hardware floor and programme partner rather than a rival hub.
- **Terra Industries.** Terra manufactures in Abuja (15,000 sq ft) and is building in Ghana (34,000 sq ft). Pitch Lagos talent pipeline, prototyping, test and PCB services, and component sourcing, not occupancy.
- **Development partners and government.** France, JICA, UNDP, Siemens Stiftung, NITDA and NASENI all fund labs, but outside Lagos or inside universities. Lagos's draft ₦31bn fund and LASRIC have no shared-lab instrument yet. Ask LASRIC or LSETF to co-fund open-access hours or equipment, the "South African model" where government funds equipment and the operator runs it.

### 5.3 Mistakes to avoid (each tied to a comparable)
1. **No single funder above about 30-40% of revenue.** Gearbox died at about 90%. (The threshold is my judgement, not a sourced benchmark.)
2. **Don't rely on memberships alone.** Build earned revenue from services from day one, as TechShop, Nesta and Gearbox all point to: PCB/assembly runs, contract prototyping, paid certification courses, corporate innovation programmes priced like FabCafe's (from about $9k), component margin.
3. **Don't buy real estate or over-build.** One TechShop cost $2.5-3.5M; Artisan's Asylum's campus left it with negative net assets. Lease and fit out in phases.
4. **Keep it open, not programme-only.** GE Garage is the local cautionary tale.
5. **Budget upkeep and spares explicitly**: 8-10% of machine capex per year, up to 15-20% conservatively (§4.6). Stock filament and acrylic locally (UNILAG lesson).
6. **Staff for technical coverage.** Fab Foundation guidance implies 4-5 technical people for two shifts. Our 1-2 technical managers plus AI tools must be backed by trained floor stewards, limited machine hours, or certified-member self-service.
7. **Plan for fabrication novelty to fade.** Programme the space with challenges, courses and corporate projects so machines don't gather dust (FabCafe; UNILAG).
8. **Wet lab: start at BSL-1, training-led and sponsor-funded** (Hive Biolab, Genspace and BioCurious economics). Don't copy LabCentral-style bench rental without VC-backed biotech tenants.

### 5.4 Open questions to verify next (not covered here)
- Lagos electronic-component and PCB supply options (Computer Village, online distributors) and their prices. This shapes the store's margin story.
- Actual opening hours, fees and utilization of the UNILAG FabLab and NCAIR FabLab, by site visit or call. Free public labs set a price anchor for us.
- Status of the Design Week Lagos hub, NG_HUB (CcHUB/Facebook, 2018) and any LASU, YABATECH or Covenant labs.
- Nigerian biosafety rules for a community BSL-1 lab (out of scope here).
- Café One unit economics (not public).
