Risks

What could go wrong, and the plan for it.

One funder

Ten revenue streams from day one, none above a fifth of the total.

Heavy buildings

One lean site in a partner’s space, with leased solar.

Upfront rent

A partner’s building, or a lease of one year at most.

Broken machines

About 12% of equipment value set aside each year, spares on the shelf, faults flagged early.

Diesel and the naira

Solar and battery first. Equipment bought once, at launch.

Safety

Inductions per machine, BSL-1 lab work only, ducted extraction, safe battery storage.

Partners

Built with partners, not around one.

A bank

A café network, solar finance, and the first account and loan of every founder who walks in.

A university

Timetabled practical hours for students, without building labs at every study centre.

Hardware companies

Sponsored seats and bootcamps that train the technicians they need to hire.

The state

Equipment grants for a shared lab any Lagos founder can use.

Makers of tools

Printers, boards and instruments placed where thousands of builders see them every month.

Investors

A Friday night pipeline of tested prototypes and the teams behind them.

Roadmap

Open in about six months.

Months 0 to 3Partners and building agreed. Design signed off. Long-lead equipment ordered.
Months 3 to 6Fit-out, solar and power. Fourteen staff hired and trained. Café and parts shop open first.
Month 6The lab opens. First bootcamp and the first Demo Night.
Month 18 onOnce the first site is profitable, a second city on the same plan and software.

We are looking for a building, about $300k for equipment and fit-out, and programmes that fill the benches from day one.
